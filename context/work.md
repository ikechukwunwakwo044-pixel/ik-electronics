# Business and Work

## Business

IK Electronics and Gadgets is a growing retail business that sells electronics, gadgets, home and kitchen appliances, entertainment products, and office equipment.

Revenue mainly comes from direct product sales. Exact revenue figures can be added later when IK wants to track them.

## Products and Revenue Streams

- **Televisions:** TVs from brands such as Hisense and other popular brands.
- **Home appliances:** Blenders, electric juicers, household appliances, and other useful home products.
- **Home entertainment:** Home theaters, speakers, and audio equipment.
- **Electronics and gadgets:** Phone accessories, electronic gadgets, and useful tech products.
- **Office equipment:** Equipment and accessories for homes, offices, and businesses.
- **Social-media sales:** Products are promoted through TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp, then converted through customer conversations.
- **Product bundles and combo deals:** Related products are packaged together as attractive offers.

## Daily Tools

- WhatsApp for customer communication, follow-up, and closing sales.
- TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook for product marketing and content.
- Canva for creating marketing materials and content assets.
- Google tools, phone, and computer for daily business operations.
