# Team

IK has a small team that supports day-to-day business operations, including sales, customer service, product handling, deliveries, and social-media activity.

## Key Roles

- **Business Owner and Manager, IK:** Oversees the business, makes important decisions, and manages sales, products, customers, marketing, and operations.
- **Sales and Customer Service:** Handles customer inquiries, follows up with interested customers, and helps convert inquiries into sales.
- **Content and Social Media:** Helps create and post product content on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp to attract customers and generate sales.

## Communication

- Main channel: WhatsApp.
- Urgent issues: Phone calls.
- Customer and marketing channels: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and WhatsApp.

## Team Management Pain Point

The main challenge is keeping everyone organized so tasks, inquiries, orders, follow-ups, deliveries, and product promotions are handled on time.

IK wants better visibility into what each person is working on so nothing gets missed.
